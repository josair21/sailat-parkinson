ABOUT THIS VIEWER

Inspect stored results. Keep interpretation careful.

Predictions, annotations and source signals are presented for research inspection. A1/A2 disagreements remain separate; probability and waveform appearance alone do not establish a labeling error.

Consensus metrics use events with matching binary labels. Balanced accuracy is not applicable for a single-class patient. Confidence flags are inspection heuristics, not clinical thresholds.
